Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 1RM?+
Your one-rep max (1RM) is the maximum weight you can lift with proper form. It is used to set training percentages and track strength progress.
Which 1RM formula is most accurate?+
Brzycki and Epley are the most commonly used and tend to be accurate for 1-10 reps. All formulas become less accurate above 10 reps. For best accuracy, test with a weight you can lift 3-5 times.
How do I train using my 1RM?+
Common training zones: Strength: 80-90%, 3-5 reps. Hypertrophy: 65-80%, 8-12 reps. Endurance: 50-65%, 15-20 reps. Power: 50-70%, 3-6 explosive reps.
